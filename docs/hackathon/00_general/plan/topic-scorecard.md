# Topic Scorecard

## Scoring rule
Rate each topic on a 1–5 scale.

**Status:** provisional. These scores are a prep heuristic, not a verified benchmark. Do not treat them as final until the source inventory is filled in.

- **Public urgency**
- **Dual-city evidence**
- **Official-source availability**
- **Joinability**
- **PR feasibility**
- **AI can stay secondary**
- **Ethics / privacy risk**

## Current recommendation

### Primary: Heat island + vulnerable groups
Why it wins:
- easiest to tell on a map,
- most natural fit for dual-city public value,
- lower privacy risk than health/mental-health topics,
- AI can remain a summary / prioritization layer.

### Backup: Disaster evacuation + shelter/material allocation
Why it is the best fallback:
- clear government action scenario,
- strong public value,
- still easy to explain to judges,
- but the scope must stay narrow.

### Stretch: Elder-care + medical capacity mismatch
Use only if joinability is unexpectedly good.

## Gate 1 pass/fail questions
Before a topic moves forward, answer all of these:

1. Who is the decision user?
2. What decision changes because of this dashboard?
3. What is the official-source basis?
4. Where is the dual-city value?
5. Can the story survive without AI?

If any answer is weak, the topic does not pass Gate 1.
