# Backup Topic Spec — Disaster Evacuation + Shelter / Material Allocation

## Problem statement
When heavy rain, flooding, typhoons, or compound hazards hit, the challenge is not simply that data exists in separate places. The real problem is that:

- vulnerable populations,
- shelters,
- transport disruption,
- and material / staffing availability

are hard to view together in one decision frame.

## Decision user
- disaster response staff,
- district operations teams,
- social welfare planners,
- emergency coordination staff.

## Decision question
> Which districts need shelter, transport, or supply support first during a hazard event?

## Why this is a strong backup
- very government-friendly,
- strong cross-city support story,
- easy for judges to understand,
- clear operational decision value.

## Main risk
Scope can blow up fast.

Keep the MVP focused on:
- one hazard type,
- one decision question,
- one response ranking,
- one explanation panel.
