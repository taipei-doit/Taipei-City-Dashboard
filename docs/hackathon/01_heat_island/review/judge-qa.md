# Judge Q&A

## Q1. Why is this dual-city?
Because Taipei and New Taipei form one living circle, but risk and resources are not evenly distributed. The dashboard compares both cities with the same indicators.

## Q2. Why this topic and not a generic city dashboard?
Because it answers one concrete public decision: where to prioritize response first.

## Q3. What is the AI doing?
Only summary, comparison, explanation, and guided interpretation.

## Q4. What happens if the AI is removed?
The dashboard still works. The charts, map, and prioritization logic remain valid.

## Q5. What can be PR’d back to the city dashboard?
A small reusable module such as a prioritization card, map overlay, comparison table, or explanation panel.

## Q6. What is the biggest risk?
Joinability and scope. If the data cannot join cleanly, the topic must be narrowed.

## Q7. Why not pick youth mental health?
It is valuable, but the privacy / ethics / data-boundary risk is higher for a hackathon first launch.
